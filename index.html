Before you hand over money

AutoTempest filters clean title for you. On the other sites, toggle the title / no-accident filter once you land — then run this checklist.

  • Run the VIN through a history report — confirm the title is actually clean, not just claimed.
  • Check for consistent service records, especially regular oil changes.
  • In Florida, check hard for rust underneath and any sign of flood / water damage.
  • Get a pre-purchase inspection from an independent mechanic ($100–150, worth it).
  • Start it cold if you can — after it's sat overnight — and listen.
