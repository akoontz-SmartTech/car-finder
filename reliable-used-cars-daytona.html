Before you hand over money

  • Run the VIN through a history report — confirm the title is actually clean, not just claimed.
  • Check for clean, consistent service records, especially oil changes.
  • In Florida, look hard for rust underneath and any signs of flood/water damage.
  • Get a pre-purchase inspection from an independent mechanic ($100–150, worth every dollar).
  • Test drive cold — start it yourself after it's sat overnight if you can.
A clean title means the vehicle has never been declared a total loss by an insurer — it excludes salvage, rebuilt, and flood-branded titles. This sheet is a starting point for the Daytona Beach / Volusia County area; verify all prices, mileage, and title status directly with the seller.
